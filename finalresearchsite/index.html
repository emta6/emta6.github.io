The Study

On Instagram, likes are public and saves are private. I interviewed five participants to understand how that affects what they choose to engage with.

Each interview became a unique lighting object. The height, the pattern, the layers — all generated from their data.

Participant A

Age 21
Pronouns He / Him
Total Likes 11,434
Total Saves 1,831
Overlap between both 57.1%

Interview Highlights

How would you describe the version of yourself you present on Instagram?

"There are like a couple of versions of myself… and I think that they are dependent on the time of day. During the day I interact with cool things that are related to my profession. At night… the content becomes really emotional and vulnerable and like deep seated in my insecurity."

Is there content you save that you wouldn’t engage with publicly? If so, why?

"When other people are around, I’m not so vulnerable. When I do see things like that resonate, because it’s just me, I feel comfortable enough to be vulnerable to save it. In that process, my saved posts are a space to be really honest with myself."
