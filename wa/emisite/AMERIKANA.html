Amerikana Lamp

Inspired by the iconic Shoji rice paper screens and Midcentury Modern textiles. A blend of Japanese traditional and American modern styles makes the Amerikana lamp a statement. Constructed with laser-cut MDF, rice paper, and bent acrylic panels to give a floated effect.
