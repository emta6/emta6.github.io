Amerikana Lamp | December, 2023

Through this project, I wanted to create a lamp that represented the two sides of my heritage. Amerikana stems from the word "America" and "Kana" which is a type of Japanese writing. I used the traditional shoji screen form mixed with midcentury modern colors and prints to develop a lamp that shows those two cultures.

Final Prototype

I leveraged 3D-modeling softwares such as Rhino to develop the lamp framing and colored pieces. I created this final prototype using laser-cut MDF, acrylic, and tissue paper.
