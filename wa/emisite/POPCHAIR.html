Pop Chair • Industrial Design • 2024

Inspired by Danish Flatpack furniture, I wanted to create a chair that packs flat but doesn't use hardware. I did this by creating a chair that uses leather joints that naturally stretch with a user's body weight.

Chair Closed

Plywood featuring leather joints allow the chair to open and close.

Outcome

Through the development of this chair, I learned how challenging yet rewarding it is to redesign a seemingly simple object such as a chair. Learning about the mechanics of a chair, while creating an innovative new design made this design exciting.
