My Process

For this soap dishes, I wanted to explore the capabilities of sustainable design. This soap dish is made entirely out of repurposed shampoo bottles and milk gallon jugs. The form was CNC'd and then the plastic is pressed into the mold to create the iconic Ripple shape.

Final Prototype

These are photos of the first two soap dishes that I created using my CNC mold. Their ripples naturally channel water away from soap in an aesthetically pleasing manner.

Outcome

This project embodies what it means to be truly sustainable product design. All of the materials would have otherwise been thrown in a landfill. Being able to create local relationships with hair salons, recycling centers, and our overall local community in order to make this project possible was rewarding for me.
